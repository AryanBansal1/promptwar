# Saathi – 3-minute demo script

**Before recording:** open the deployed URL in Chrome on an Android phone (or desktop Chrome at 360 px width) in a fresh profile, so onboarding shows and no entries exist. Allow the microphone when asked. Prepare one real screenshot of your UPI/Google Pay history or a platform earnings screen. Keep narration short.

| Time | Screen | Action | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home (light theme) | Show the warm theme, the onboarding card, Ask Saathi with its 🎤 button, and the red emergency bar. | "Saathi helps gig and daily-wage workers. No login, six languages, and you can speak instead of typing." |
| 0:10 | Onboarding → **My Work** | Tap **Set up now**. Tick **Delivery** and **Bike taxi**; tick **Zomato**, **Rapido**, **Blinkit** → **Save**. | "Many workers do more than one job. Saathi remembers this on the phone only." |
| 0:22 | Language | Switch to **हिन्दी**. | "Everything follows the language, including the AI." |
| 0:26 | Home – Ask Saathi | Tap 🎤 and **say**: *"आज Swiggy से 800 कमाए और 200 का पेट्रोल डाला"*. Words appear live. Tap 🎤 again to stop, then tap **पूछें**. | "Voice uses the phone's speech engine in Hindi; it only fills the box, it never sends by itself." |
| 0:40 | Confirmation card | Show **"क्या ये एंट्री जोड़ें?"** with ▲ +₹800 income and ▼ −₹200 fuel. Tap **सेव करें**. Show "Today so far" and **वापस लें (10)**. | "Gemini understood Hinglish and two amounts; the date 'aaj' is worked out in code. Nothing is saved until I tap Save, and I can undo." |
| 0:55 | **आय और ख़र्च** | Open Income & Expenses. Show both entries labelled "please check", plus the summary: income, expense, net, today, by platform, **by work type**. | "All maths is plain Python on the server, not the AI." |
| 1:05 | Import | In **Import**, choose your UPI screenshot → **AI से फ़ाइल पढ़ें**. Show the review: rows, flags for low confidence or missing date. Untick one row, edit an amount → **Save selected**. | "Gemini reads the screenshot, but every row is checked by me first. The file is not stored." |
| 1:25 | Theme | Tap **डार्क मोड**. Show green + and red − amounts still clear. | "A dark mode for night shifts. Colours always come with + and − and arrows." |
| 1:32 | Complaint from an entry | On the Zomato income entry tap **पैसे नहीं मिले / ग़लत भुगतान की शिकायत**. Form opens pre-filled. Switch language to **ਪੰਜਾਬੀ**, add a description by 🎤 in Punjabi, then draft. | "From any payment straight to a complaint, now in Punjabi." |
| 1:50 | Letter actions | Show the English and Punjabi letters. Tap **ਈਮੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਭੇਜੋ** (opens mail to Zomato's grievance officer), then **WhatsApp 'ਤੇ ਭੇਜੋ**, **Copy**, **Download**. Show **Where to send it**. | "Email goes only to an address confirmed on Zomato's own site. Where to send it comes from verified data, not the AI." |
| 2:10 | Safety | Open **ਸੁਰੱਖਿਆ**, tap **ਤੰਗ-ਪਰੇਸ਼ਾਨੀ** → send. Show steps and **ਸ਼ਿਕਾਇਤ ਲਿਖੋ**. | "Safety advice fits the work types I chose; helplines are hardcoded below." |
| 2:22 | Schemes → Documents | **ਸਕੀਮਾਂ**: age 30, income 12000, work types pre-ticked from My Work, Punjab, bank **Yes** → find. Show the explanation, then **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕਿਹੜੇ ਦਸਤਾਵੇਜ਼ ਚਾਹੀਦੇ ਹਨ?** → checklist, tick one. | "Eligibility is rules in code; Gemini only explains matched schemes." |
| 2:40 | Honest errors | Turn off Wi-Fi, tap any AI button → translated error with **Try again**. Turn Wi-Fi on. | "If AI fails, Saathi says so; it never shows pre-written answers." |
| 2:50 | End | Show the emergency bar. | "Saathi: one helper for money, safety, rights, papers and complaints, by voice or text." |
